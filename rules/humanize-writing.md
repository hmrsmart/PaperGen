# Humanize Writing Rules for Academic Research Papers

This rule set defines the standards for all prose generation, rewriting, peer critique, and section synthesis in PaperGen.

## 1. Vocabulary and Phrasing Prohibitions
Avoid typical AI filler words and cliches:
- **Banned phrases**: "delve into", "at its core", "in the realm of", "it's important to note", "crucial role in shaping", "today's fast-paced world/digital age", "sheds light on", "underscores", "plays a pivotal role", "stands as a testament to", "boasts", "a treasure trove of", "rich tapestry", "game-changing", "seamless", "rapid proliferation of", "remarkable advances".
- **Superlatives to cut**: "captivating", "majestic", "fascinating", "groundbreaking" (unless presenting verified historical breakthrough).
- **Vague hedge-attributions**: Never write "some experts believe" or "studies have shown" without naming the specific authors and year `[1, 2]`.

## 2. Structural & Rhetorical Rules
- **No negative parallelism**: Avoid "It's not just X, it's Y" and "not only X, but also Y". State claims directly.
- **Break the rule of three**: Do not automatically group adjectives, metrics, or examples in triplets. Use a single precise word, or real lists of 2, 4, or 5 items.
- **No inflated significance**: Do not artificially inflate results ("marked a pivotal moment in science", "solidified its legacy"). Report exact numbers plainly: accuracy deltas (+3.3%), throughput (1,890 samples/sec), latency (0.8ms), memory reduction (42%).
- **No formulaic over-transitioning**: Avoid starting paragraphs with "Furthermore,", "Moreover,", "Additionally,", "In conclusion,", "Overall,". Start with the concrete subject or argument.
- **Vary sentence cadence**: Balance short, punchy statements with longer technical explanations. Do not produce uniform-length sentences.
- **Direct, unhedged limitations**: State failure modes, hardware bounds, and computational costs directly without evasive sugar-coating.
